# EBG Creative — Cold Outreach Email Campaign
**Target:** Small business owners & startups
**Goal:** Book discovery calls
**Sequence:** 3 emails over ~12 days

---

## STRATEGY NOTE

You're unknown — so don't lead with yourself. Lead with *their* problem.
Every small business owner wonders why their brand doesn't feel like *them* anymore.
That's the door in. EBG's job in this sequence is to make them feel seen, get curious, and take one small action.

## EMAIL 1 — THE OPENER
**Send:** Day 1
**Goal:** Get the open. Get the click. One ask only.

---

**SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS** *(A/B test these — pick one per batch)*

- `your brand is lying about you`
- `quick question about [Business Name]`
- `why does your brand still look like 2021?`
- `honest question for [First Name]`

---

**BODY**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I looked at [Business Name] and I think you're underselling yourself.
>
> Not the product — the product is clearly good. But the brand? It's not keeping up with you.
>
> Most small businesses start with something quick and functional. A logo from a template, a colour palette that felt fine at the time, fonts they've never thought about since. And then the business grows, gets better, finds its voice — but the brand stays frozen at where it started.
>
> The result: people meet your business online before they meet you. And what they see doesn't match what you've actually built.
>
> I'm Ella, the founder of EBG Creative. We help small businesses and startups build brands that actually reflect who they are — not who they were when they first started.
>
> Before you commit to anything, I built something you can try for free in about 5 minutes: **[The Playground](https://ebgcreative.com)** — drop in your colours, pick your fonts, see what your brand could look like. No form. No pitch. Just play.
>
> If it sparks something, I'd love to talk.
>
> — Ella
> EBG Creative | hello@ebgcreative.com

---

**WHY THIS WORKS**
- Opens with an observation about *them*, not a pitch about you
- "I looked at your business" signals effort, not mass email
- Positions the Playground as a low-friction entry point — no commitment required
- The ask is small: try a free tool, not "hire me"

## QUICK TIPS FOR SENDING

**Personalize the opener line.** The more Email 1 feels like you actually looked at their business, the higher your open-to-reply rate. Even one specific detail ("I noticed you recently rebranded your Instagram" or "I love what you're doing with [product line]") can double response rates.

**Batch sizes.** Send in small batches of 10–20 at a time so replies don't overwhelm you and you can refine as you go.

**Don't send all 3 to everyone at once.** Only move someone to Email 2 if they haven't replied to Email 1. If they reply at any point — pull them out of the sequence and respond personally.

**Track opens if you can.** Tools like Streak (Gmail plugin, free) let you see who opened without clicking. If someone opens 3 times but doesn't reply, that's a warm lead — follow up manually.

**Subject line split test.** Try 2 different subject lines across your first batch and track open rates. Double down on the winner.
